Krishna likes talking about literature because stories preserve experience and reveal different ways of seeing the world. Philosophy provides space to question assumptions, values, meaning, and how a life should be lived.

Cinema brings those interests together through image, sound, performance, and storytelling. Psychology adds another perspective by exploring how people think, feel, behave, and relate to one another. These are not separate from engineering: they help Krishna build with greater curiosity, empathy, and attention to the human experience.
